How many squares are on a chessboard?

Most people say 64. Those are only the smallest ones. A block of 2 by 2 cells is a square too, and so is 3 by 3, all the way up to the whole board. Count them yourself below.

How many squares are on a chessboard? · Brain vs Math

The answer: 204

A square of 2 by 2 cells can start in 7 columns and 7 rows, so there are 7 × 7 = 49 of them. A 3 by 3 square fits 6 × 6 = 36 ways, a 4 by 4 square 25 ways, and so on until the single 8 by 8 square that is the whole board.

64 + 49 + 36 + 25 + 16 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 204.

In general a square of size k fits (n − k + 1) times across and (n − k + 1) times down an n by n board, so the total is 1² + 2² + … + n², which is n(n + 1)(2n + 1) ÷ 6. For n = 8 that is 8 × 9 × 17 ÷ 6 = 204. A 10 by 10 board holds 385.

A harder one: how many rectangles?

Every rectangle on the board is fixed by two of the 9 vertical grid lines and two of the 9 horizontal ones. There are 36 ways to choose two lines from nine, so the board holds 36 × 36 = 1,296 rectangles, and the 204 squares are among them.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't the answer 64?

64 counts only the single cells. Any block of cells that is as wide as it is tall is also a square, so the 2 by 2, 3 by 3 and larger blocks add 140 more.

How many squares are on a 10 by 10 grid?

10 × 11 × 21 ÷ 6 = 385. Pick the 10 by 10 board above to step through it.

Do rectangles that are not squares count?

Not in this puzzle. Counting every rectangle gives 1,296 on a chessboard.
